The 5-Phase Scenography

A disciplined, chronological progression transmuting personal iconography and emotional tone into an unrepeatable physical reality.

01 Weeks 1 – 2

Discovery & Alignment

We begin not with vendor spreadsheets, but with a forensic excavation of your sensibilities. Through our bespoke aesthetic questionnaire and immersive salon sessions, we calibrate emotional moodboards, construct precise capital allocations, and present the foundational vision deck.

  • Bespoke Patrons' Aesthetic & Sensory Audit
  • Comprehensive Budget Architecture & Liquidity Roadmap
  • First-Edition Creative Codex & Color Harmonics
02 Weeks 3 – 6

Creative Direction & Spatial Design

Abstract aesthetics transmute into spatial dimensions. We engineer three-dimensional venue models, calibrate custom botanical palettes with master florists, test tactile textiles against ambient lighting, and create physical tabletop mockups in our private Paris and London studios.

  • Volumetric 3D Venue Scenography & Traffic Kinetics
  • Bespoke Botanical Formulations & Olfactory Accords
  • Tactile Tabletop Prototype Reviews with Hand-Cut Crystal
03 Months 2 – 6

Meticulous Planning & Sourcing

The operational machine engages with clinical accuracy. We arbitrate elite international contracts, architect seamless multi-destination guest travel movements, convene bespoke multi-course culinary and mixology tastings, and commission tailored performance art pieces.

  • Michelin-Caliber Culinary Concepting & Sommelier Pairings
  • High-Discretion VIP Travel Protocols & Private Charters
  • Curated Acoustic Lineups & Custom Orchestrations
04 Final 30 Days

Coordination & Rehearsal

Every contingency is mapped, stress-tested, and memorized. Our production leads draft the master run-of-show calibrated to the minute, manage comprehensive load-in schedules across all technical trades, and conduct closed-door rehearsal walk-throughs to ensure total composure.

  • Minute-by-Minute Synchronized Master Run-of-Show
  • Full Engineering Load-In & Acoustic Sound Audits
  • Choreographed Private Rehearsal & Host Walkthrough
05 Execution Day

Event-Day Execution

On the celebratory day, our presence is omnipresent yet completely invisible. We steer lighting shifts according to the solar arc, direct discreet backstage culinary transitions, and anticipate the comfort of every patron from the first morning sunrise to the final late-night teardown.

  • Discreet Ghost Production Team with Shadow Management
  • Dynamic Solar & Candlelight Ambience Regulation
  • White-Glove Archival Preservation & Post-Event Teardown
